# Open questions register

Consolidated register of every open question in chapters 01-10: the items listed in each chapter's `## Open questions` section plus the unresolved items and disputes in each `## Verification log`. **155 entries: 65 open, 85 resolved, 5 retired** (last resolution pass 2026-09-16).

This register is authoritative for status. Where a chapter's own `## Open questions` list still carries an item that is marked `resolved` or `retired` here, the status here wins; each chapter's `## Verification log` records the resolution under `Resolution 2026-09-16`.

The `status` column takes `open`, `resolved` or `retired`. `resolved` means the question has an answer the rulebook now encodes; `retired` means the question is withdrawn, either because it is unaskable as put or because the decision behind it is settled and will not be revisited. A narrowed question stays `open` with its text rewritten to what actually remains. The `resolution` column gives the method, the evidence in one line, and the date.

The 2026-09-16 pass rests on six evidence files under [`evidence/`](evidence/): `corpus-variants.md` (40 usage-variant and source-conflict questions), `lexical-lists.md` (16 lexical-list questions, with the generated lists under `local/lists/`), `productivity-tagset.md` (Tolerance Principle verdicts and the UD/UniMorph decisions), `calibration.md` (measured thresholds), `norm-gaps.md` (the advisory archive, offline) and `norm-gaps-online.md` (primary legal, academic and corpus evidence found online for the questions the archive could not settle).

`OQ-CC-NNN` IDs are stable. `NNN` matches the chapter's own numbering where the chapter numbers its open questions (`OQ-03-004` = `NOM-OPEN-4`, `OQ-05-003` = chapter 05 open question 3); log-derived items continue the chapter's sequence. Retired IDs are never reused: `OQ-02-011`, `OQ-07-002` and `OQ-08-006` are retired because those list items are marked resolved in their chapters.

Categories (no others are used):

- `usage-variant`: which of two or more standard-ish forms is preferred, and how often
- `norm-gap`: AkH or another norm does not state it, or its text and examples contradict
- `lexical-list`: a closed-class membership list is incomplete or unverified
- `source-conflict`: two reputable descriptive sources disagree
- `tagset-mapping`: a UD/UniMorph value is unclear
- `statistical-estimate`: a weight, threshold or rate in the rulebook is a guess and needs measuring
- `theory-productivity`: whether a process is productive, or how to model it
- `engineering-choice`: our own design decision, no external truth

Impact: `blocking` = an implementation cannot produce or judge a common form without deciding; `quality` = affects correctness of less common forms or lint precision; `cosmetic`.

Rows are grouped by category, and within a category sorted by impact then chapter.
